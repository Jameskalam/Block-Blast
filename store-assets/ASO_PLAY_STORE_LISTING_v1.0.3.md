## 3. Full Description (3,420 Characters / 4,000 Maximum)

```text
Welcome to Block Mint: Puzzle Game, a clean, relaxing, and mentally stimulating block puzzle game designed for players of all ages. If you enjoy classic block blast puzzles, grid matching challenges, and strategic brain puzzles, Block Mint delivers a crisp, ad-friendly, and offline-ready experience.

Whether you want to unwind after a long day or keep your mind sharp during your commute, Block Mint provides endless entertainment with zero time limits, no forced pressure, and completely offline gameplay.

=======================================================
HOW TO PLAY BLOCK MINT
=======================================================
Playing Block Mint: Puzzle Game is simple to pick up, yet deeply rewarding to master:

1. Drag and Place Shapes: Choose from three distinct geometric block pieces in your tray and place them anywhere on the 8x8 grid.
2. Form Complete Lines: Fill complete horizontal rows or vertical columns to blast blocks off the board and clear space.
3. Plan Ahead: Blocks cannot be rotated, requiring thoughtful spatial planning and forward thinking to ensure you never run out of room.
4. Keep the Board Clear: The puzzle game ends when there is no available space remaining for any of the three current pieces.

=======================================================
KEY GAMEPLAY FEATURES
=======================================================

• Dynamic Combo Multipliers:
Clear multiple rows or columns in a single move, or make consecutive line blasts on back-to-back turns to trigger COMBO multipliers. Watch your score multiply as your streak grows.

• Collectible Theme Shop:
Earn gold coins simply by clearing lines, reaching high scores, and achieving combos. Use your coins in the Theme Shop to unlock handcrafted visual styles including Ocean Breeze, Cyber Neon, Golden Sunset, and Emerald Forest.

• Free Daily Rewards:
Launch the game daily to collect bonus coins and keep your coin bank growing.

• Second-Chance Board Rescue:
When you find yourself out of moves, enjoy an optional second-chance revive that clears central board space so you can continue your record-breaking run.

• Integrated In-App Tutorial:
New to block puzzles? Access our interactive 3-step walkthrough anytime from the Settings menu or main screen to brush up on placement strategies and combo mechanics.

=======================================================
WHY PLAY BLOCK MINT?
=======================================================

• Train Your Brain:
Exercise your spatial awareness, pattern recognition, and logical problem-solving skills with every piece placed.

• Stress-Free & Relaxing:
No ticking clocks, no countdown timers, and no high-stress penalties. Play at your own natural pace and enjoy crisp audio cues and satisfying visual blast effects.

• 100% Offline Friendly:
Enjoy the full block puzzle game wherever life takes you. No Wi-Fi, mobile data, or internet connection is required to play the core game.

• Privacy-First Design:
No user accounts, social media logins, or passwords required. Your high scores, statistics, and unlocked themes are stored locally on your device.

• Lightweight & Battery Optimized:
Engineered for smooth performance across all modern Android devices and screen sizes without draining your battery or consuming excessive storage.

=======================================================
PRO TIPS FOR HIGHER SCORES
=======================================================
- Always leave an open 3x3 square in the center of the board for large square pieces.
- Avoid placing single blocks in isolated corners where they block complete rows.
- Build up lines and trigger double or triple clears simultaneously for big score bonuses.
- Keep your combo streak alive by clearing at least one line on every consecutive placement.

Download Block Mint: Puzzle Game today and start blasting blocks!
```
