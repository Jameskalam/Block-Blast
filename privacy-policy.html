Privacy Policy for Block Mint

Effective Date: October 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Block Mint ("we", "our", or "the Game") handles your information. Block Mint is designed as an offline-first casual block puzzle game.

1. Information Collection and Use

Block Mint does not require any user account, registration, login, email address, or name to play. All game progress, high scores, coins, and themes are stored locally on your device.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Block Mint uses third-party advertising services provided by Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to serve banner and rewarded video ads. These third-party services may automatically collect certain device information, such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (GAID / AAID)
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device identifiers, operating system version, and device performance metrics
  • Ad interaction and diagnostic data for ad delivery and fraud prevention

For more details on how Google uses data from apps that use its services, visit: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

3. Children's Privacy

Block Mint does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps.

4. Data Security

All game data is maintained locally on your device. Any information transmitted by third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) is encrypted in transit using standard HTTPS/TLS encryption.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
