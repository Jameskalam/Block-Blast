Privacy Policy

Blockmint (Android) · Effective 2 September 2026

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Blockmint, a block-puzzle game for Android. It is written to be read, not to hide behind legal language.

The short version. We do not have any servers and we do not collect, store or transmit any personal information about you. Your game progress stays on your own device. The only data that ever leaves your phone is collected by Google AdMob, which shows the ads that keep the game free.

1. Information we collect

We collect nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no servers of our own. We never see your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files or precise location.

2. Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally so it is still there next time you play. This information never leaves your phone and is not visible to us:

WhatWhy
High score, best combo, games playedTo show your records
Coins and power-up countsSo purchases and rewards persist
Level reached and unlocked themesTo restore your progress
Daily streak and last reward dateTo work out your daily bonus

Deleting the app, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, permanently erases all of it. There is no cloud copy, so it cannot be recovered.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is funded by ads supplied by Google AdMob. To choose and measure ads, Google may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • your IP address, and an approximate (city-level) location derived from it
  • device information such as model, operating system and language
  • whether an ad was shown, viewed or tapped

This collection is performed by Google inside its advertising SDK. We do not receive this data; we only see anonymous, aggregated totals such as how many ads were shown in a day.

Google's handling of this data is governed by its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Non-personalised ads only

Because this game may appeal to a general audience including children, we have configured AdMob to request non-personalised ads and to treat all traffic as child-directed. In practice this means:

  • ads are chosen from general context, not from a profile of your interests
  • ad content is restricted to a family-friendly rating
  • we do not enable interest-based or remarketing advertising

5. Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. The app requires no registration, collects no personal details, and contains no chat, social features, user-generated content or links to social networks.

The game does show advertisements and offers optional rewarded ads, which are configured as described in section 4. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about this, please contact us using the details below.

6. In-app purchases

This version of the game contains no in-app purchases and asks for no payment details. Coins are earned by playing or by choosing to watch a rewarded advertisement, and hold no real-world monetary value.

The app does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, storage, precise location or phone identity. We have explicitly removed the microphone and storage permissions that our audio library would otherwise have added, because the game does not need them.

8. Your choices

  • Reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Play offline — turn off your connection and no ads will load.
  • Delete all your data — uninstall the app, or clear its storage.
  • Mute audio and haptics — use the sound button in the game.

9. Data retention

We hold no data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. Data on your device is kept until you remove the app. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's own retention policies.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this same address with an updated effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's Play Store release notes.
